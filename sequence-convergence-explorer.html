Sequence Convergence Explorer

Explore the definition of convergence: for every ε > 0, there is an N such that |aₙ − L| < ε whenever n ≥ N.

Examples: 1/n, (-1)^n, (-1)^n/n, 2+1/n, sin(n)/n. Use ^ for powers.
